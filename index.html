CORE FIELD SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
Requirements
  • Hard hats, safety glasses, and steel-toe boots required at all times.
  • Fall protection required at heights of 6 feet or greater.
  • No site-built scaffolding permitted.
  • All wall panels and trusses must be properly braced.
  • Unsafe conditions must be corrected immediately.
Fall Protection Enforcement
  • 1st Offense: $500 per person
  • 2nd Offense: $1,000 + 1 month suspension
  • 3rd Offense: $1,500 + 1 month suspension
  • 4th Offense: $2,000 + 1 year suspension
  • 5th Offense: Removal from BFS approved list
Site Safety Plan
Safety Violations
  • Hardhat – Glasses – Boots: $50–$100 per person
  • Ladder Violation: $100–$250 per person
  • Safety Rails: $100–$250 per person
  • Crane Safety Violation: $100–$500 per offense
  • Drugs or Alcohol: $500 – Immediate Termination
Fall Protection
  • 1st Offense: $500 per person
  • 2nd Offense: $1,000 + 1 month suspension
  • 3rd Offense: $1,500 + 1 month suspension
  • 4th Offense: $2,000 + 1 year suspension
  • 5th Offense: Removal from BFS approved list
Wind/Storm Protocol
  • Wall panels and trusses must be properly braced at all times.
  • Loose materials must be secured prior to storm events.
  • High wind warnings require immediate jobsite review.
  • If materials fall due to improper bracing, automatic rebuild required.
  • Superintendent must be notified immediately of weather damage.
Stop Work Authority

Work must be paused if unsafe weather conditions develop. No production schedule overrides safety requirements.

Worker Safety
  • Propane heaters are strictly prohibited.
  • Burn piles are not permitted on job sites.
  • Open flames are not allowed within framed structures.
  • Temporary heating equipment must comply with OSHA standards.
Worker Protection
  • Proper cold weather PPE must be worn.
  • Hydration must be maintained even in cold conditions.

SITE & SUBCONTRACTOR DUTIES
Materials
  • Do NOT move materials from one lot to another without approval.
  • Unauthorized transfers may be treated as theft.
  • Damaged materials must be reported before installation.
  • Forklift operators must be trained and certified.
Storage
  • Materials must be stacked securely and level.
  • Jobsite access must remain clear at all times.
Foundation Review
  1. Notify builder or project manager immediately if discrepancies are found.
  2. Document issue with written notice and photographs.
  3. Determine corrective solution before continuing work.

Failure to notify before continuing work will result in subcontractor responsibility for repair costs.

Site Safety
  • Maintain clean and hazard-free work areas.
  • All OSHA, state, and local safety regulations must be followed.
A. Requirements
  • Subcontractor is 100% responsible for the safety of its employees, suppliers, and sub-tier subcontractors.
  • All personnel must comply with the BFS Safety Management Program.
  • Unsafe acts or conditions must be corrected immediately.
  • BFS reserves the right to remove any worker violating safety requirements.
Competent Person
  • Each subcontractor must designate a Competent Person.
  • The Competent Person must have authority to correct hazards immediately.
  • The Competent Person must conduct regular site inspections.
  • The Competent Person must ensure compliance with fall protection and OSHA standards.
Supervisor Duties
  • Supervisors are responsible for enforcing all safety rules.
  • Supervisors must lead by example.
  • Supervisors must stop unsafe work immediately.

SAFETY PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS
Required Docs
  • Subcontractor must maintain a written Safety & Health Plan.
  • Fall protection plan required for heights 6 ft or greater.
  • Hazard Communication program must be in place.
  • SDS documentation must be available onsite.
  • Equipment inspection logs must be maintained.
Safety Training
  • All workers must receive task-specific training.
  • Fall protection training required before working at height.
  • Ladder safety training required.
  • Equipment operators must be certified.
  • New employees must complete orientation prior to working onsite.
Injury/Incident Reporting
  • All injuries must be reported immediately.
  • Serious incidents require immediate notification to BFS management.
  • Written incident report must be submitted within 24 hours.
  • Root cause analysis required for recordable incidents.
Code of Safe Practices
  • No horseplay or unsafe behavior permitted.
  • Follow all OSHA, state, and local safety regulations.
  • Respect stop-work authority.
  • Report hazards immediately.
Fire Prevention
  • No smoking inside framed structures.
  • Flammable spills must be cleaned immediately.
  • Fire extinguishers must remain accessible.
  • Temporary power must be inspected and compliant.
General Enforcement

Violations of this safety program may result in removal from the project, suspension from future work, or permanent removal from the BFS approved subcontractor list.
